Hello fellow explorers,

I am Aqsa Shaikh, PhD candidate in Mitas' Group, Department of Physics, NCSU.
This repository contains an overview of my research and a collection of academic research publications, technical reports, and selected coursework
in computational material science, computational chemistry, quantum information and related fields.

# Research Interests:
- AI for Scientific discoveries
- Designing computational workflows for highly correlated systems
- Quantum Information and Quantum Computing
- Statistical Modeling and Optimizations using Neural Networks
- Molecular and Material Simulations with Benchmark results

# Scientific Computing & Research Tools:
Due to the vast nature of my research requiring cross-validation of various theoretical predictions,
I have gained expertise in variety of software packages, to list a few:

- **Electronic Structure & Quantum Chemistry:** PySCF, Molpro, GAMESS, DIRAC, OPIUM
- **Density Functional Theory & Materials Simulation:** Quantum ESPRESSO
- **Conventional Quantum Monte Carlo (QMC):** QMCPACK, CASINO
- **Neural-Network Quantum Monte Carlo (NNQMC):** FermiNet, DeepQMC, DeepErwin, DeepSolid, JaQMC
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning:** PyTorch
- **Programming & Scripting:** Python, Bash, Shell Scripting
- **High-Performance Computing (HPC):** SLURM, GPU/CPU computing, cluster environments
